Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your CatchIQ account and associated data at any time. Once confirmed, your account will be permanently removed within 30 days.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to support@catchiq.fishing from the address associated with your account.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete My Account.
  3. We will confirm your identity and send a confirmation email within 2 business days.
  4. Once confirmed, your account and data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Data Action
Account profile (name, email, photo) Deleted
Catch records and photos Deleted
Fishing spots Deleted
Group memberships and invites Deleted
Tournament entries and scores Deleted
Subscription billing records Kept 7 years (tax / legal requirement)
