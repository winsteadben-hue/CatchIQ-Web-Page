About IQ.Fishing

Built by anglers,
for anglers.

IQ.Fishing is an intelligent fishing companion that helps recreational and competitive anglers log catches, read conditions, find their best spots, and run tournaments — all in one app. We believe every angler deserves access to the kind of data and decision support that used to require a team of guides and years of local knowledge.

Our Mission

We're building the most useful fishing app on the market — one that works as hard as you do. Not a simple logbook. Not a weather overlay. A fully integrated intelligence layer that combines your personal catch history with real-time environmental data to tell you where to fish, when to go, and how to get there.

IQ.Fishing captures tide phase, barometric pressure, solunar periods, moon phase, and wind automatically — at the moment of every catch. Over time, your data becomes a predictive model unique to you and your water. Pick any future date and the map shows you probability scores for every spot you've ever fished.

Tournament Platform

IQ.Fishing includes a full tournament management platform for clubs, guides, and competitive anglers. Organizers can create tournaments, collect entry fees securely via Stripe, and run a live scoreboard — all without leaving the app.

Anglers who have expressed interest in participating can request a registration link from the organizer. Upon request, they receive an email containing tournament details and a secure payment link. Once payment is complete, they are automatically registered and can begin submitting catches within the tournament window. All catches go through TrueCatch verification before appearing on the scoreboard.

IQ.Fishing sends tournament registration emails on behalf of organizers through our secure, AWS-hosted email infrastructure. Emails are only sent to anglers who have requested to participate and whose contact information has been provided to the organizer by the angler themselves.
