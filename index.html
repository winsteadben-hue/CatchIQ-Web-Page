How It Works

From photo to prediction
in one app.

01

Photograph Your Catch

Take a photo of any fish. IQ.Fishing reads the EXIF metadata to extract the exact time and GPS location, identifies the species with on-device AI, and estimates length and weight — all in under 60 seconds, with no manual input.

02

Conditions Are Captured Automatically

As each catch is logged, IQ.Fishing fetches the tide state, weather snapshot, moon phase, and solunar score for that exact location and moment. The catch pin appears on your nautical chart, surrounded by the conditions that produced it.

03

Patterns Emerge

Your personal catch history builds a dataset unique to you and your spots. The analytics dashboard surfaces patterns — which tides, pressures, and moon phases your best catches share. The heat map shows where the action concentrates.

04

Pick a Date. See the Map.

Select any future date and the prediction map lights up — every saved spot shows a colour-coded probability score based on forecast conditions matched against your historical data. Tap the best spot and plan your trip.

For Tournament Organizers

Run your tournament
entirely in the app.

01
🏆

Create & Configure

Set up your tournament with species rules, date window, entry fee, and scoring format — all from the Manage tab. No paperwork, no spreadsheets.

02
✉️

Share Registration Links by Email

Add anglers one at a time or upload a CSV roster. Anglers who have requested to participate receive an email with a secure registration and payment link — no IQ.Fishing account required to pay entry.

03
💳

Entry Fees via Stripe

Connect your Stripe account once. Entry fees go directly to your account at checkout — IQ.Fishing deducts a small $2.00 per-entry platform fee. No manual invoicing required.

04
✅

Auto-Registered on Payment

When an angler completes checkout, they are automatically registered and marked as paid in your roster. No manual tracking. Live scoreboard updates in real time as catches come in.

05
📸

TrueCatch Verified Submissions

Tournament catches go through TrueCatch — a 7-layer automated verification system. EXIF timestamps, GPS location, AI species recognition, and a measurement instrument check ensure every submission is clean and fair.

06
📊

Live Scoreboard

A real-time scoreboard is available to all participants. Rankings update automatically. At the end of your tournament window, winners are clear — no manual tabulation needed.
